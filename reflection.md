## Reflection

This project reinforced my understanding of Hadoop Streaming and the MapReduce 
programming paradigm. By converting a Java-based tutorial into a Python solution,
 I practiced designing lightweight scripts for distributed computing and learned
 how to integrate them into HDFS workflows. 

I deepened my understanding of HDFS operations, key-value mapping, and the reduce 
phase aggregation process. Troubleshooting the differences in syntax and ensuring
HDFS compatibility made the learning experience more hands-on and practical. It also 
gave me insight into how distributed processing pipelines function under the hood.
